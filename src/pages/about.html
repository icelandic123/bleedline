About Bleedline

Most rejected book covers and blurry shirts come from the same few mistakes: the wrong spine width, missing bleed, too few pixels, or a fee nobody counted. Bleedline is a set of small tools that catch those mistakes before you upload.

How the tools work

  • Everything runs in your browser. Images and PDFs are made on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, so your artwork and manuscripts stay private.
  • Specs come from the platforms. Each calculator links to the help pages its numbers come from and shows when they were last checked. Platforms change their rules, so check their page before you publish.
  • Free first. The tools are free with no sign-up. Pro adds bulk features for people who publish a lot, and it pays for keeping the specs current.

Something wrong or missing?

If a number looks off or you'd like a tool that isn't here, please say so. Corrections with a link to the platform's page get fixed first.{{contact}}
