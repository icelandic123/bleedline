Privacy

Your files

Every image, PDF and calculation is processed in your browser. Bleedline has no server that receives your files, and none are uploaded.

What your browser stores

Your tool settings are saved in your browser's local storage so they're still there next time. If you unlock Pro, your license key is stored the same way. You can clear both by clearing this site's data in your browser.

What is sent elsewhere

  • Pro license checks. When you unlock Pro, your key is sent to Gumroad to confirm the purchase, and checked again about once a week. Purchases themselves happen on Gumroad under Gumroad's privacy policy.
  • Visitor counts. The site may count page views with a cookie-free analytics service, which records the page, referrer, browser type and country, not who you are.

Bleedline does not sell data, show ads, or use tracking cookies.
